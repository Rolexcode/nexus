# Nexus demo script

## 90-second version

**0:00–0:15 — Problem**

“On campus, finding useful information and reporting a physical problem often happen in the same place: scattered WhatsApp groups and word of mouth. Nexus gives LASU Epe one location-aware layer for both discovery and accountability.”

**0:15–0:30 — Discover**

Open **Explore campus**. Search for a place or practical service and open its map details.

“Students can first use Nexus for something ordinary: finding where things are and what is available.”

**0:30–0:50 — Report + confirm**

Open **Report & track**. Submit a prepared incident. On a second account/device, attest to it.

“When something is wrong, the same map becomes a reporting layer. The issue is tied to a place, the reporter can stay publicly anonymous, and another campus account can confirm what they actually observed.”

**0:50–1:10 — Campus intelligence**

Open **Admin view** and select the new report on the heatmap.

“Officials no longer receive isolated complaints. They see where reports cluster, how serious they are, how much community confirmation exists, and what needs action.”

**1:10–1:25 — Close the loop**

Move the status through **Verified → In progress → Resolved**, then return to the student report view.

“The important part is what happens after submission. Students can see that the problem moved from complaint to action.”

**1:25–1:30 — Close**

“Nexus turns campus information into campus intelligence: discover it, report it, confirm it, resolve it.”

## Demo preparation

- Keep one browser/device signed into the reporter demo account.
- Keep another browser/device ready for the attestation demo.
- Use a short incident title with an obvious location.
- If Firebase is enabled, refresh the second device after the first device writes so it pulls the latest shared demo state.
- Keep Admin View ready in another tab so the switch feels instant.
- Do not spend demo time on provider listing forms unless a judge asks.
